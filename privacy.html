Privacy Statement

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Krash Games ("we", "us") makes games. This statement explains how we handle information in our games and on this website.

Our games

Our games do not collect, store or share any personal information. They have no accounts, analytics, advertising or crash reporting. Game progress and settings are saved only on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the game.

This statement covers all our games, including Kindred.

If a future game collects any information, its own policy will say so before it is released, and this statement will be updated.

App stores

When you download our games, the store you use (for example Google Play) handles that process under its own privacy policy. We receive only anonymous, aggregated statistics such as download counts.

This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking scripts. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.

Contacting us

If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply to you. We do not share them with anyone, and we delete them once they are no longer needed.

Children

Our games are suitable for all ages. Because they collect no personal information, they collect none from children.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access or delete personal information we hold about you. In practice, the only information we could hold is an email you sent us. To ask us to delete it, contact us at the address below.

Changes

If this statement changes, the date above will be updated.
