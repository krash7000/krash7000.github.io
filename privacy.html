Privacy Statement

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Krash Games ("we", "us", "our") makes games. This statement explains what information we collect when you play our games or visit this website, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us – for example, your email address and message when you contact us for support.
  • Gameplay and device data – such as game progress, settings, device type, operating system and anonymous crash reports, used to make the game work and fix problems.
  • Third-party services – app stores and platforms (e.g. Steam, Google Play, Apple App Store) may collect information under their own privacy policies.

We do not knowingly sell your personal information.

How we use information

  • To provide, maintain and improve our games.
  • To respond to your questions and support requests.
  • To diagnose bugs, crashes and performance issues.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

Sharing

We only share information with service providers who help us run our games (such as hosting or crash reporting), when required by law, or with your consent.

Data retention

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes above. Support emails are deleted once they are no longer needed.

Children

Our games are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal information, or to object to how we use it. To make a request, email privacy@krashgames.com.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking scripts.

Changes

We may update this statement from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows when it was last changed.
